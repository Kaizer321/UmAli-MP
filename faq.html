HELP CENTRE

Good questions deserve clear answers.

UmAli is still preparing for launch. These answers describe the current preview, not live service guarantees.

Is UmAli live?

No. The marketplace is in preparation and does not have live inventory, real checkout or active account services.

Why are there no products?

We do not want to present fictional products, prices, sellers or reviews. Real inventory will appear only after onboarding.

Can I become a seller?

The seller studio is a preview of the tools being prepared. Real onboarding will require a connected account and verification.

How will buyer protection work?

Protection will be based on clear offer details, visible order status, evidence and a published resolution process.

Can I use the forms?

Forms are visual previews only. No personal data is saved or transmitted from this static prototype.

Where can I share feedback?

Use the contact page to describe what would make a digital marketplace more useful to you.
